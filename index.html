How can we help?

Contact us with bug reports, privacy requests, or suggestions.

yusahmedia@gmail.com

For bug reports, include your iPhone model, iOS and app versions, and the steps that led to the issue. Hide private information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, session tokens, or precise location information.

Why can’t I see my friend on the map?

Your friend request must be accepted. Your friend must enable both location permission and in-app location sharing, and have an internet connection to send fresh measurements. Optional background sharing additionally requires the in-app switch and Always location permission; updates are not guaranteed after force quitting.

Can I sign in on a new phone using my username?

An email-free account relies on the secure session stored on your device. Username-only account recovery is not available in the current version. Keep this limitation in mind before losing or signing out of that session.

Are speed and traffic information exact?

No. GPS, connectivity, and map provider data can introduce delays or errors. Vehicle status is an estimate. ST LOC is not an emergency or safety monitoring system. Do not interact with your phone while driving.
